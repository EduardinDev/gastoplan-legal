2. The honest summary

GastoPlan runs entirely on your device. We have no servers and we never receive your data.

The application contains no network client whatsoever: it is technically incapable of sending your information anywhere. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking tools.

Everything you type — your plans, your expenses, your categories and your name — is stored only in your phone's memory.

4. In-app purchases

GastoPlan offers a single one-time purchase, GastoPlan Pro, which unlocks unlimited exports and unlimited plans.

Payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play).

  • We never see or store your payment method, card number, address or purchaser identity.
  • The application receives only a confirmation from the operating system that the purchase exists, so it can unlock the feature.
  • We receive aggregated sales reports from Apple and Google, which cannot identify you.

Apple and Google act as independent controllers for the data they collect to process the payment. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies, not by this one.

6. Legal basis for processing

We are established in Ecuador, so the Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP) applies to us. Because we offer the application to consumers in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also applies to the extent relevant.

Under either regime:

  • Your name, financial data and preferences are processed on your own device and under your exclusive control, in order to provide the service you installed. The basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), which you enter into by using the application.
  • We carry out no consent-based processing for advertising, analytics or profiling, because we carry out none of those activities.

Since we never receive your data in any system of ours, our ability to process it is, in practice, non-existent.

9. Deleting your data

There are no user accounts, so there is no account to delete.

To erase all your data:

  • iOS: uninstall the application.
  • Android: uninstall it, or go to Settings → Apps → GastoPlan → Storage → Clear data.

Deletion is immediate and complete on your device. We keep no copies, because we never had any.

Your purchase history stays associated with your Apple or Google account, not with ours: that is what lets you restore GastoPlan Pro if you reinstall.

10. Children

GastoPlan is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from minors. Since there are no accounts and no server-side data collection, the application processes no personal data from any user, regardless of age.

11. Security

Your data lives in the application's private storage, protected by the app sandboxing mechanisms provided by iOS and Android.

Because the information never travels over the network and is never hosted on servers of ours, there is no remote attack surface against it.

We cannot guarantee absolute security against a compromised, lost or unlocked device. We recommend keeping your system updated and using your device's screen lock.
